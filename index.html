Your Hydrex votes, working for the climate

Carbon Impact is an automation for Hydrex accounts. It votes with your accounts every week, retires a share of the rewards as carbon credits and pays you the rest. Your HYDX never leaves your wallet.

Anyone can check it: its code is Hydrex's own, it retires carbon credits through Klima, and this page checks it on Base before it lets you join.

  • Its votes count for nothing. Hydrex counts a vote only in the epoch it is cast, and the automation has not voted since.

How it works, in three steps

  1. It votes with your accounts every week, just before the epoch ends on Thursday at 00:00 UTC.
  2. It claims their rewards right after, and swaps them into kVCM, Klima Protocol's token backed by carbon credits.
  3. It retires a share of the kVCM as carbon credits, and sends you the rest.

A join takes effect in two steps: Carbon Impact can claim your account's rewards at once, and votes with it from the next epoch, which starts on Thursday at 00:00 UTC.

Questions

Can I still vote myself?

Not with an account in Carbon Impact: from the epoch after it joins, its votes count for Carbon Impact. Leave, and you vote with it again from the next epoch. Accounts you keep out vote as before.

What happens to rewards I have not claimed?

Carbon Impact can claim them from the moment an account joins, even those your own votes earned, and swaps and retires them like the rest. To keep them as they are, claim them on Hydrex before you join.

What carbon credits, and where can I see them?

Carbon Impact retires them through Klima, in the name "Hydrex", which is the same for every member. Each retirement is a transaction of the automation, on Basescan.

Can a DAO join from a Safe?

Yes. In Safe{Wallet}, add https://metadex.cafe/ as a custom Safe App. The page proposes one Safe transaction for all the accounts, and the Safe's owners sign it in Safe{Wallet}.
