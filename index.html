The Carbon Impact automation

An automation for Hydrex accounts. Every epoch, it votes with the accounts in it, then claims their rewards, swaps them into kVCM, retires a share as carbon credits and pays the rest to their owners. One admin key controls its settings.

      What joining allows

      From the contracts' current settings:

      • The Carbon Impact automation votes with the account from the next epoch, which starts on … at 00:00 UTC. Once the account joins, you can't vote with it yourself, until the epoch after it leaves.
      • It swaps, retires and pays. Right after each epoch ends, the Carbon Impact automation claims the rewards. It swaps them into kVCM through approved routers, retires … of it as carbon credits in the name "…", and sends the rest to your payout address. It takes no fee today.Its fee is … today. Swaps have their usual costs.
      • The Carbon Impact automation can claim the account's rewards from the moment it joins, even those your own votes earned. That is any you have not claimed yet, and this epoch's once the epoch ends. They are swapped and retired like the rest. To keep them as they are, claim them on Hydrex before joining.
      • Your HYDX stays in your account, and the account stays in your wallet. Neither is transferred.
      • The automation's admin key can change these settings: the share retired (up to 100%), the tokens it pays out in, its fee on the payouts (up to 1%), the routers, and your payout address.
      • You can leave at any time. From that moment the Carbon Impact automation can no longer claim the account's rewards; what it has not claimed is yours to claim on Hydrex. Your votes come back to you from the next epoch; until then they count for the Carbon Impact automation.
