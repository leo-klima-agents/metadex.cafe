      What joining allows

      From the contracts' current settings:

      • The Carbon Impact automation votes with the account from the next week, which starts on Thursday at 00:00 UTC. Meanwhile you can't vote with it yourself.
      • The Carbon Impact automation can claim the account's rewards from the moment it joins, including any you have not claimed from past weeks. Claim them on Hydrex first to keep them as they are.
      • It swaps, retires and pays. Right after each week ends, Klima's keeper claims the rewards. The Carbon Impact automation swaps them into kVCM (Klima Protocol kVCM) through routers Hydrex approved, retires … of it as carbon credits in the name "…", and sends the rest to your payout address. Hydrex takes no fee today.Hydrex's fee is … today. Swaps have their usual costs.
      • Your HYDX stays in your account, and the account stays in your wallet. Nothing is transferred.
      • Hydrex can change these settings: the share retired (up to 100%), the payout tokens, the fee (up to 1%), the routers, and your payout address.
      • You can leave at any time, with one transaction per account. From that moment the Carbon Impact automation can no longer claim the account's rewards; what it has not claimed is yours to claim on Hydrex. Your votes come back to you from the next week.
