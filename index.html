How it works, in three steps

  1. It votes with your accounts every week, just before the epoch ends on Thursday at 00:00 UTC.
  2. It claims their rewards right after, and swaps them into kVCM, Klima Protocol's token backed by carbon credits.
  3. It retires a share of the kVCM as carbon credits, and sends you the rest.

A join takes effect in two steps: Carbon Impact can claim your account's rewards at once, and votes with it from the next epoch, which starts on Thursday at 00:00 UTC. When things happen

                      What joining allows

                      Your account stays in your wallet. Joining approves AutoMaxi to vote with it and to claim its rewards, which it pays to your own address. There is no payout address to change.

                      While you are in, anyone can claim your rewards through AutoMaxi, at any time, and the fee is the same whoever calls. Its keeper, Carbon Impact's, claims right after each epoch ends. Rewards nobody has claimed through it yet, you can still claim on Hydrex yourself.

                      If you join now

                      1. Now. Anyone can claim your account's rewards through AutoMaxi, which pays them to you.
                      2. At the next flip, Thursday at 00:00 UTC. AutoMaxi votes with your account. Anyone can propose its votes: it takes one only if it expects it to earn at least $1 more in bribes, counting WETH, cbBTC, USDC and EURC at Chainlink's prices, and some trading fees in other tokens at their pool's average price over the last half hour. A week without a vote earns nothing.
                      3. After each epoch ends. Its keeper claims the rewards, and AutoMaxi sends them to you, less 1%.

                      Who controls what

                      • Nobody controls AutoMaxi. A new fee, treasury or price feed means a new deployment.
                      • Hydrex can upgrade its Voter, VotingEscrow and reward contracts, so AutoMaxi is only as safe as they are.
                      • The owner of Hydrex's reward contracts can deposit a bribe, win the vote with it and take it back, or take any bribe's tokens.
                      • Chainlink's prices only steer its votes. If a feed goes stale, its token counts for nothing, and a worse vote can win while it lasts.
                      • Hydrex's pools price the trading fees in other tokens, and they too only steer its votes. Someone who moves a thin pool's price, or Hydrex, which can change how its pools report prices, can make a worse vote win. A pool's fees in other tokens never count for more than its fees in the four tokens above.

                      What it means for an account still in one

                      • Its votes count for nothing. Hydrex counts a vote only in the epoch it is cast, and the automation has not voted since.
                      • It earns no fees or incentives from voting, since the epoch that began on Thu 10 Sep.
                      • You can't vote with it while the automation holds its votes, and the automation can still claim its rewards.
                      • What the automation left unclaimed is yours to claim on Hydrex: small amounts, from before it stopped. Nothing has accrued since.

                      What you can do

                      Two ways out, both from this page, and both one call per account:

                      1. Leave the automation, and vote yourself. Its access ends at once, and your votes come back to you from the next epoch: vote with them on Hydrex every week.
                      2. Join Carbon Impact. It replaces the stopped automation's access directly, and votes with the account from the next epoch.

                      Either way, the votes move at the next epoch, which starts on Thursday at 00:00 UTC. To keep what the stopped automation left unclaimed, claim it on Hydrex first.

                      What joining allows

                      Joining is one call per account, which approves Carbon Impact for two things only: voting with the account, and claiming its rewards. The review before your wallet asks shows the same, for the accounts you picked.

                      It votes with the account

                      From the next epoch, which starts on Thursday at 00:00 UTC. Once the account joins, you can't vote with it yourself, until the epoch after it leaves. It votes for the pools it picks, the same for every account in it.

                      It can claim the account's rewards at once, even those your own votes earned

                      From the moment an account joins, Carbon Impact can claim any rewards it has not claimed yet, and this epoch's once the epoch ends, whoever's votes earned them. They are swapped and retired like the rest. To keep them as they are, claim them on Hydrex before you join.

                      It swaps, retires and pays

                      Right after each epoch ends, Carbon Impact claims the rewards and swaps them into kVCM, through routers its admin key approved. It retires a share of the kVCM as carbon credits, and sends the rest to your payout address. Swaps have their usual costs. The carbon credits

                      You can leave at any time

                      Leaving is the same call with false, and works even if this page's checks fail. From that moment Carbon Impact can no longer claim the account's rewards: what it has not claimed, this epoch's included, is yours to claim on Hydrex. Your votes come back to you from the next epoch; until then they count for Carbon Impact.

                      When things happen

                      An epoch runs for a week, from Thursday at 00:00 UTC. Two rules decide everything else: claims move at once, in the same transaction as a join or a leave, and votes move at the next epoch, since Hydrex counts an account's votes for whoever held them when the epoch started.

                      Join on a Monday

                      1. Monday, as you join. Carbon Impact can claim the account's rewards from now on, including any you have not claimed. This epoch's votes stay as they were: yours, or the automation's it was in.
                      2. Wednesday, just before 24:00 UTC. Carbon Impact votes with its accounts, but not yet with yours.
                      3. Thursday, 00:00 UTC. The next epoch starts. The account's votes now count for Carbon Impact, and you can't vote with it.
                      4. Thursday, right after. Carbon Impact claims the rewards of the epoch that ended. It can claim your account's too, which your own votes earned, and swaps, retires and pays them like the rest.
                      5. The next Wednesday, just before the end. Carbon Impact votes with your account for the first time.
                      6. The Thursday after, right after 00:00 UTC. The first payout from its votes: a share retired, the rest to your payout address in kVCM. Then every Thursday.

                      Join on a Thursday

                      The same, a week longer. Join on Thursday at 10:00 UTC, right after an epoch starts, and the votes move only at the Thursday after: almost a week later than joining on the Wednesday before. Join on Wednesday at 23:00 UTC, and they move an hour later.

                      Leave

                      Claims stop at once: Carbon Impact can no longer claim the account's rewards. Its votes count for Carbon Impact until the epoch ends, and what they earn is yours to claim on Hydrex after the flip. From Thursday at 00:00 UTC, you vote with the account again, or choose another automation.

                      Hydrex's own app has said that a change of automation takes effect "after the next payout". On Base, claims move at once and votes from the next epoch, as above.

                      Who controls what

                      Carbon Impact is a contract of Hydrex's, whose source is in Hydrex's repository. Two roles act on it.

                      The keeper

                      It votes with the Earning Power of every account in Carbon Impact, and claims, swaps, retires and pays out each account. It picks the pools and the swaps, through routers the admin key approved. It picks how much to retire, up to the share set of the kVCM: the contract stops it retiring more.

                      The admin key

                      It can change, at any time and for every member at once:

                      • the share retired, up to 100%;
                      • its fee on the payouts, up to 1%, and where the fee goes;
                      • the tokens it pays out in, and the routers it swaps through;
                      • the carbon credit retired, and the name and message on each retirement;
                      • any member's payout address;
                      • what joining approves, and who holds either role.

                      It can also withdraw any tokens the automation holds.

                      What neither can do

                      Move your account or your HYDX. What joining approves is voting and claiming; the account stays yours, and leaving ends both.

                      How a change would show

                      Every change is a transaction from the admin key to the automation, on Basescan. This page reads the share retired and the name on the retirements from the automation each time it opens, and shows them as they are. It checks what joining approves, and turns joining off if that changed.

                      This page names no operator, and asks you to trust no one: what it says is read from Base, and anyone can check it.

                      The carbon credits

                      kVCM is Klima Protocol's token, backed by carbon credits. Carbon Impact swaps the rewards into kVCM, and pays you in it. You can keep it, retire it yourself, or swap it on Base, as on Hydrex.

                      Retiring a carbon credit uses it up for good: no one can sell it or claim it again. Each payout, Carbon Impact retires a share of the kVCM as carbon credits through Klima. It has retired … tonnes so far.

                      Your accounts stay as they are, and Carbon Impact goes on. Leaving is one call on Hydrex's VotingEscrow, which any interface that can call a contract, such as Basescan's, can make.

                      Words

                      Account
                      A Hydrex account: HYDX locked in Hydrex's VotingEscrow, which gives it Earning Power. It is a token in your wallet.
                      Flex Account
                      An account whose lock ends, or rolls on so that it never does.
                      Protocol Account
                      An account locked for good, as from oHYDX or rebases.
                      Earning Power
                      An account's weight in Hydrex's votes. Hydrex shows it as 1.3 times the votes the contracts count.
                      Epoch
                      Hydrex's week, from Thursday at 00:00 UTC. Votes count for one epoch, and rewards are paid by epoch.
                      The flip
                      The moment one epoch ends and the next starts.
                      Automation
                      A contract that votes and claims for the accounts that approve it. Hydrex's contracts call it a conduit.
                      Keeper
                      Who runs an automation's votes and payouts each week.
                      Admin key
                      The key that can change an automation's settings. Who controls what
                      Payout address
                      Where Carbon Impact sends an owner's payouts: the owner's own address, unless changed.
                      kVCM
                      Klima Protocol's token, backed by carbon credits, in which Carbon Impact pays.
                      Carbon credit
                      A certificate for a tonne of carbon dioxide kept out of the air, or taken from it.
                      Retire
                      To use a carbon credit up for good, so that no one can sell or claim it again.
