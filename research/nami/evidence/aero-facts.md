## Headline findings

1. **Aero is live and most supply has migrated.** New AERO total supply is 2,143,046,806. Of that, 1,077,274,170 (50.3%)
   is staked as sAERO, and 334.6M still sits unclaimed in the two migration contracts.
2. **Aero's emission is a continuous per-second rate, not a schedule.** The Minter's global rate is 13.489345 AERO/s
   (8.16M/week), which is exactly 20% a year of the 2.127B pre-mint. Tokens mint only when LPs claim. Realized minting
   so far is about 53% of that ceiling.
3. **The 20% ceiling is not immutable.** The operator Safe can move the base rate by up to 1.2% per 47 hours today, and
   the contract's hard limit is 5% per week. Governance can also mint the "never minted" surplus through
   `Voter.spendSurplus`.
4. **Aero admin power is concentrated.** One 3-of-7 Safe holds every role on the Voter and LeafVoter, including
   governance and emergency council. A second Safe with the same seven owners is the Minter operator. Neither Safe has a
   module, guard or timelock.
5. **Gauge creation on Aero is governance-gated on chain**, despite the docs calling it permissionless. Base has 311
   gauges for 977 pools, and the only registered creation modules require `GOVERNANCE_ROLE`.
6. **Gauge caps are set off chain.** On the CL gauge factory, cap operators are the operator Safe and one
   EIP-7702-delegated EOA, within a range of 0.0001 to 7.5 AERO/s per gauge.
7. **Legacy Aerodrome's "Aero Fed" was not a token-holder vote at the end.** The epoch governor before migration was
   `SimpleEpochGovernor`, where only the team multisig could set the result. The tail rate sat at 0.21% a week.
8. **Legacy Aerodrome still trades, without emissions.** It holds about $109M TVL and did about $807M of volume in the
   last 7 days, alongside Aero's $6.05B. Its sampled gauges are killed.

# Part 4. Open questions and items I could not verify

1. **Original Aerodrome tokenomics post.** Medium returned HTTP 403. The six-bucket split is from the current docs; the
   finer split of the 95M "Development Team Funding" bucket is unconfirmed.
2. **History of the legacy tail rate.** I confirmed 67 bps in code and 21 bps before migration, but not how or when it
   moved, nor whether a vote-based `EpochGovernor` ever operated.
3. **Safe signers.** Owner addresses are readable, but who they are is not published in what I read. The two Aero Safes
   share all seven owners.
4. **Identity of Splitter recipient 0xc59B7390…EA5F** (3-of-5 Safe). Its five owners are all owners of the Public Goods
   Fund Safe. The docs say the share goes to the Foundation.
5. **Hyperlane security.** I did not check the ISM, validator set or relayer arrangement behind root-to-leaf messages,
   or whether Metalane is entirely Hyperlane.
6. **Leaf chains.** All parameter reads are on Base. Cooldown, max gauges, unstaked fee, MEV tax and caps on other
   networks were not read. Chain ids 4663, 5042 and 99901 were not matched to names.
7. **Velodrome side.** The OP Mainnet migration contract and final VELO supply were not read on chain.
8. **Audit reports.** I listed them from the docs but did not read the PDFs or their findings. The Sherlock bounty page
   was not opened.
9. **Token Registry.** The unit of the 1e20 deposit and how tokens are actually listed on Base are unclear; `nextId()` =
   1 suggests the request path is unused.
10. **sAERO minimum period at contract level**, and when merge and split will ship.
11. **Third-party lending or marketplaces** for veAERO or sAERO were not researched.
12. **Relay factories.** The USDC Maxi relay reports factory 0xA5283582…4B78, while the docs list RelayFactory
    0xA972232e…049d. Both are named `RelayFactory`; I did not reconcile them.
13. **Docs versus chain mismatches worth raising with the team:** permissionless gauge creation; "never minted" surplus
    versus `spendSurplus`; separate Emergency Council; 7-day minimum incentive duration; tick spacing list; contributor
    share "staked as a Perpetual Stake".
14. **Realized inflation.** Mint-on-claim makes any short window noisy. The 7-day figure (14.0% annualized) is above the
    8–12% the team said to expect; the since-launch figure is 11.0%.
15. **Perpetual Cyclist Services LLC.** Named as licensor and copyright holder; its relation to the Delaware "Dromos"
    LLC in the Blockworks filing is not stated.
