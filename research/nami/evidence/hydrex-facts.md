## 0. The ten things that matter most

1. **Permanent locks burn the HYDX.** The ERC-20 supply is only 107.4M. Another 592.7M HYDX was burned into permanent
   veHYDX. Total voting power is 474.3M. `[V]`
2. **A permanent lock gets 1/1.3 votes per HYDX; a 2-year Flex lock gets 1 vote per HYDX.** The UI's "Earning Power" is
   votes × 1.3, a display convention. `[V]`
3. **The Treasury Safe (2-of-3) holds 59.0% of all voting power** and has not cast a gauge vote since 2026-09-16. `[V]`
4. **Emissions are now 300,000 HYDX a week, all to gauges, paid as oHYDX.** Rebase and the 4% team/treasury cut are both
   zero on chain since week 52. `[V]`
5. **Gauged Integral pools send 100% of swap fees to voters** (98.5% after Algebra's 1.5%). LPs in gauged pools earn
   emissions only. `[V]`
6. **LP emissions on manual concentrated positions are settled off chain**: a Hydrex indexer publishes Merkle roots
   every 30 minutes. `[V]` contract, `[D]` cadence.
7. **Upgrades go through a 24-hour timelock, but many levers do not**: the Ops Safe (2-of-4) and one EOA (`0x7426…AE03`)
   hold direct powers over fees, whitelists, bribes, the option token and the emission base. `[V]`
8. **Token-holder governance is real but narrow**: an OpenZeppelin Governor and a weekly emissions vote control the tail
   emission rate and the reserve contract. They do not control upgrades. `[V]`
9. **Base only.** No HYDX or veHYDX bridging. "Synths" bring Solana assets to Base with Hydrex's own bridge, which is a
   separate product. `[V]` token and escrow code, `[D]` Synths.
10. **Small today**: TVL $2.84M (peak $23.4M in January 2026), about $10M weekly volume, about 0.12% of Base DEX volume.
    `[S]`

## 15. Note for the local repo (not changed)

`/home/user/metadex.cafe/src/rewards.ts:286-287` says "A vote of a permanent lock stands for one HYDX". On chain a
permanent lock gives 1/1.3 votes per HYDX burned, so one vote of a permanent lock stands for 1.3 HYDX (or 1.3 oHYDX). If
the APR there is meant as a return on HYDX committed, it is overstated by a factor of 1.3; if it is meant per unit of
the lock's stored `amount`, it is right.
